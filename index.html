About Me

Caleb is a stand-up comic, writer, and producer making smart, character-driven comedy.

As a stand-up, he's featured for Jenny Zigrino (Dropout.TV, Sirius XM) and opened for BBC shortlisted comic Lady Bushra, performing at Cobb's Comedy Club, The Stand, Gotham Comedy Club, ColdTowne Theater, Edinburgh Fringe, Netflix is a Joke Fest, and Vice City Comedy Festival. He's also competing in Rooster's Comedy Competition.

As a writer, executive producer, and performer, he co-created Luigi the Musical — a dark comedy that sold 3,000+ tickets, sparked a national media moment, and earned coverage from The New York Times, NPR's All Things Considered, The Hollywood Reporter, Broadway World, and a mention from Stephen Colbert. More news on the project to be announced soon. His new play, The Baron, debuts at Netflix is a Joke at The Elysian alongside Jenny Zigrino. While Luigi brought national attention, Caleb continues to tour as a stand-up and remains available for club and festival bookings.

Caleb recently discovered that Type B personalities exist, which — let's be honest — is a very Type A thing to do. He was also told he's a polymath, to which he replied: "but I'm bad at math."

Available for clubs, festivals, corporate events & co-writing.
