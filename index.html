About

Queer stand-up comic, writer, actor, producer, and “big personality” best known as the co-creator of the viral sensation Luigi: The Musical (New York Times, NPR, Washington Post) and co-writer of The Drill Master with Jenny Zigrino.

Like Meringue but with a Z (no relation)

NYC Queer Comedy Festival finalist. Edinburgh Fringe. Vice City Comedy Festival. LGBTLolFest. Opened for national headliners including Jenny Zigrino and Lady Bushra. Performed at Cobb's, The Stand, Gotham, Hollywood Improv, and The Elysian.

Grew up across 16 states. Comedy is unapologetically queer with a suspicious amount of emotional vulnerability and “bless your heart” southern energy. Recently discovered that Type B personalities exist, which — let's be honest — is a very Type A thing to do.

Quick answers

For bookers, press, and anyone searching Luigi the Musical, The Drill Master, or queer comedian.

Who co-created Luigi the Musical?
Caleb Zeringue is the co-creator and original executive producer of Luigi: The Musical, the viral satirical prison musical covered by the New York Times, NPR, the Washington Post, CNN, and other outlets. The official show site is luigithemusical.info.
What is The Drill Master and who wrote it?
The Drill Master is a gay history comedy about America’s most fabulous general. It was co-written by Caleb Zeringue and Jenny Zigrino. Learn more at thedrillmaster.gay.
Is Caleb Zeringue a queer comedian?
Yes. Caleb is an openly queer stand-up comedian and writer. His work is unapologetically queer, with credits including NYC Queer Comedy Festival finalist, Edinburgh Fringe, and opening for acts such as Jenny Zigrino and Lady Bushra.
