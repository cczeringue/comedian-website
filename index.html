About Me

Caleb Zeringue is a stand-up comic, writer, actor, producer and "big personality" best known as the original Executive Producer, original cast member and co-creator of the viral sensation Luigi: The Musical which was covered in the New York Times, NPR's All Things Considered, and Washington Post just to name a few.

As a stand-up, Caleb Zeringue is an NYC Queer Comedy Festival finalist, featured at Vice City Comedy Festival and LGBTLolFest, and has opened for national headliners including Jenny Zigrino and Lady Bushra — who STILL won't talk to him. He's performed at Cobb's, The Stand, Gotham, and the Edinburgh Fringe Festival.

He grew up across 16 states (his dad insists he's not bipolar, why do you ask?!). His comedy is unapologetically queer with a suspicious amount of emotional vulnerability and "bless your heart" southern energy.

He and Jenny Zigrino (Dropout.TV, Sirius XM) co-wrote The Drill Master — the mostly true story of America's most fabulous gay general, for the 250th anniversary. Table read April 1 in San Francisco (The Eagle); staged reading May 3 at The Elysian (Netflix Is a Joke Fest), Los Angeles.

Caleb recently discovered that Type B personalities exist, which — let's be honest — is a very Type A thing to do. He was also told he's a polymath, to which he replied: "but I'm bad at math."
